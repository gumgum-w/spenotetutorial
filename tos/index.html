About App

This Application is designed to help users record and manage their spending or expenses only. It does not include any income tracking feature.

Information Collection and Use

  • Current Data Collection: SpenTracker does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal or financial information to external servers. All data created within the application remains stored locally on your device.
  • Future Updates: Should future versions of the application introduce features requiring data collection, we will update this policy accordingly and provide clear advance notice.
  • Email Communication: If you contact us directly via email, your email address and any provided information will be used solely to respond to your inquiry and support requests. We do not sell or share your contact details.

Data Storage & Liability

  • Local Ownership: Because we practice a strict privacy-first model, all transaction history, images, and logs are stored exclusively on your local device under your control.
  • User Responsibility & Disclaimer: You are solely responsible for maintaining backup copies of your local files. SpenTracker is provided "as is," and we accept no liability for any loss, corruption, or damage to your data caused by device malfunction, accidental deletion, or system failure.

Cryptocurrency Functionality & Display Limits

  • Offline Ledger Processing: SpenTracker supports manual logging of select cryptocurrency values purely as an accounting preference. We do not process, validate, store, or transmit live blockchain transactions, private keys, or wallet addresses.
  • Precision & Balance Boundaries:To maintain app stability and accurate mathematical rendering, maximum manual input and display balances are currently limited as follows:
    • BTC, DOGE, LTC: Up to 1,000.00000000 units (8-decimal precision).
    • TRX, USDC, ETH, USDT: Up to 10,000.000000 units (6-decimal precision).
  • Valuation Disclaimer: All cryptocurrency entries are tracked locally based on user-entered values or local calculations. The app does not act as a financial exchange, crypto custodian, or official wallet, and we accept no liability for discrepancies between local logs and real-time market valuations.

This data is processed according to the privacy policies of the attestation providers (e.g., Google Play Integrity).

Images

Images uploaded by users are automatically cropped to 640 x 640 pixels and may experience a slight reduction in quality to optimize performance and storage eficiency.

Access to Files and Storage

Our app uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) to allow users to access, view, and manage their own files. This means the app does not access files automatically or in the background — users must manually select the files or folders they wish to use. We do not collect, store, or share any file content unless explicitly selected by the user and only for the purpose of the app’s functionality.

Notification

Our app may request permission to send you notifications, this includes push notifications for updates, reminders, or important information related to the apps functionality.
We do not collect or store any personal data through notifications. You can manage or disable notification permissions at any time through your device's settings

PIN

Currently our app does not provide a PIN recovery feature. You are responsible for remembering your PIN. If it is lost, access to your account cannot be restored. We highly recommend saving your PIN in a secure location.

IMPORTANT NOTICE: VERSION 1.4+ DATA COMPATIBILITY

  • Architecture Upgrade Notice: Due to a total framework and data model overhaul in SpenTracker (Version 1.4 and above), database structures from versions prior to 1.4 are incompatible with this release.
