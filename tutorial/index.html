    Introduction
    Please tab on Transaction to create your first transaction

    Afterward you will see form like this :


    Date: Tap to change the date of your transaction.

    Store: Enter the store name for your transaction. This is optional and can be left blank.

    Description: Add a simple note for your transaction. This is optional and can be left blank.

    Transaction Plan: Save a transaction for the future instead of adding it directly to your history. You can view, edit, delete, or submit your saved plans from the Plans tab.
    Note:As noted in your warning notification, currency and currency position cannot be changed. The free tier allows a maximum of 3 plans.

    Transaction Plans

    The Transaction Plans option allows you to log a transaction temporarily.
    You can later choose to delete it or save it to your history. To use this, create a transaction as usual, but set the Transaction Plans option to Yes.







    Tap the Submit button, and your transaction will appear on the Transaction Plan page:




    Note : By choosing Transaction Plans, you cannot upload a receipt on the initial screen, but you can upload a receipt photo later on the Transaction Plans page. Additionally, the free version allows a maximum of 3 transaction plans.

    Tap the Save to History button to save your transaction.
    To view your saved transactions, go to the history page.
